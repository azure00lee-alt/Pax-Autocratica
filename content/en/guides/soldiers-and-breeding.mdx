## The workforce loop

Citizens track Fear, Happiness, Hunger, and Loyalty. Policies, food, healthcare, comfort, surveillance, punishment, propaganda, and feasts influence those states.

## Soldier roles and life stages

Soldiers have social interactions and relationship outcomes. The Aged state reduces movement speed and work attributes.

## The four core states

Fear, Happiness, Hunger, and Loyalty are the four states tracked for citizens. Policies, food, healthcare, comfort, surveillance, punishment, propaganda, and feasts influence those states.

## Relationships, pregnancy and adulthood

The interface shows an expected delivery date for pregnant soldiers. Adulthood begins at age 18.

## Assignment and enlistment

Adults go to Victory Square for formal enlistment. Auto-assignment prioritizes soldiers with stronger relevant work attributes.

## Common problems

When stamina does not recover correctly, check the `Time Off` recovery state.
